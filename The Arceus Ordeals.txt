<h2>✅Chapter 1 - The Premonition</h2>

<p>Rain cascaded down like the drumbeat of hunters, drenching Lumiose City. Standing in imperious vigil at the center was Prism Tower – lofty, elegant, unchanging. A reminder.</p>

<p>Jett gazed out the window at the cityscape, lost in thought.</p>

<p>Her apartment high-rise in the city heart was only a block away from the impressive historical landmark. At this hour, Prism Tower shone dazzling gold, brightly lit up in a fitting testament to Lumiose's tourist appellation as the City of Lights.</p>

<p>Jett's company, Quasartico Inc., had done many renovations on the building; first to repair deterioration as part of an urban development project she'd spearheaded – then to repair damage from a disaster that had almost overtaken the city. Today it looked no different than when it had first been built, not even hinting at the calamity Lumiose had narrowly escaped.</p>

<p><i>...or calamities yet to come,</i> she reflected.</p>

<p>It was unlike her to be so fatalistic. Nevertheless, lately she had been on edge, as if she anticipated something; <i>what</i> it could be was anyone's guess. She had not slept well in a few days. The lingering stress of Lumiose's near-miss of a catastrophe, she figured, and truthfully this seemed the most logical explanation because life had been peaceful since then.</p>

<p>The widow of the former Silph Company owner, Mrs. Jett Silph was about fifty, blonde, with sky-blue eyes. Her bearing was always refined and aristocratic, effortless in grace and public poise; this had not changed, and no one who knew her from the insincerity of social circles could have ever guessed how time and tension were subtly beginning to take their toll.</p>

<p>The phone on her desk rang at the moment. She answered it and heard a familiar voice; it was Corbeau.</p>

<p>Sebastian Corbeau, the head of Rust Industries – the presentable business front for the Rust Syndicate, which was the Kalos region's reigning crime family. He also presently held the position of Lumiose City gym leader, having been appointed a year or so ago, and was a specialist in poison type Pokémon. Jett was well-acquainted with him, and their interactions could easily be understood as a friendship. Today, however, he sounded very forthright.</p>

<p>"Jett...I need to speak with you," he said before she could say hello. "It's regarding the debt you've incurred."</p>

<p>She felt a shudder of nervousness throughout her body. He had always been incredibly cavalier in their dealings and had not even reminded her of the business favor he'd extended her, some time ago; it had been long enough for her to almost forget she owed the Rust Syndicate at all.</p>

<p>But debts were never to be taken lightly. Her late husband had done so with Team Rocket, the mafia family that reigned over a large section of the world, and it had cost him his life. The Rust Syndicate and Corbeau himself held an equally shadowy reputation, and Jett was astute enough not to try it.</p>

<p>She steeled herself and asked, "What is your order?"</p>

<p>"I'd like you to meet me at the Sushi High Roller restaurant, at 6 p.m. I have a reservation there."</p>

<p>She scribbled down the rendezvous on a piece of paper. "Then you'll tell me what you want me to do?"</p>

<p>"That is what I want you to do." </p>

<p>There was silence.</p>

<p>"Technically you don't have to come," added Corbeau. "But I won't be demanding payment anyway."</p>

<p>"Mr. Corbeau..." Jett said, tentatively, "I'm at least twenty years older than you."</p>

<p>"You're also the only person I've met in all of Kalos who shares my appreciation for unorthodox ways of conducting business. Frankly, Mrs. Silph, we have more in common with each other than not."</p>

<p>She thought this over, and then smiled gently. "Alright," she replied, "I'll meet you at 6."</p>

<p>"I'll tell you what," added Corbeau, "Instead of you driving to meet me, I'll go pick you up."</p>

<p>It was a date, and Jett hung up feeling a bit more cheerful than she had been in recent days.</p><hr>

<p>The rain had tapered off to leave puddles everywhere; Jett understood tomorrow was forecast to be bright and sunny. This was the last hurrah of summer after all, still felt in all its oppressive heat during the daytime, even as nature positioned itself to change.</p>

<p>The turn could be seen in small details. Evening brought a chill to the air, a reminder that the season was pivoting fast into autumn. The trees planted along the road verges were still green, yet their topmost boughs were starting to be etched by gold and flame. Lumiose City in autumn had a certain romanticism that even this nascent period could hint at.</p>

<p>Promptly at six, Corbeau and his limousine pulled up to Jett's building. She came to the car quickly and they set off to the restaurant.</p>

<p>Sushi High Roller was a high-class establishment tucked away in Rouge Plaza, with black-panelled decor accented with gold. It was known as much for its gourmet seafood as it was for the unique stage in the center of its dining area, where patrons were provided with entertainment from music, dance, or Pokémon battles.</p>

<p>When they arrived at the restaurant, the maitre-d greeted Corbeau warmly. "Your usual table, sir?" he asked Corbeau.</p>

<p>"Yes," he replied, "and bring a bottle of your best champagne, please."</p>

<p>As soon as they were seated, the couple were handed menus. Despite being best known for seafood as per its name, the restaurant had a varied menu; they settled on steaks with red wine. Soon the waiter came by and took their orders, and they spoke a while as they waited for the meal to be served.</p>

<p>"This was a nice surprise," said Jett.</p>

<p>"Was it?" replied Corbeau, "I've been in thinking about doing this for a long time...quite frankly I'm glad you agreed to it." He smiled, and she returned the smile.</p>

<p>Corbeau was thirty-five, always dressed in tailored black suits that complemented his lean physique, with round glasses framing sharp amber eyes and aubergine hair he kept short and combed to one side. He was popular amongst the ladies, but his heart was set on Jett.</p>

<p>Their dinner finally arrived and they settled down to eat and converse some more. As they talked, they started cutting the meat and then something extraordinary happened.</p>

<p>A piece of meat that Jett had carved off her dish seemed to be pulled away on its own, floating from the plate towards the floor, and were it not for a tell-tale tail of some sort of fluffy animal that was visible bouncing along behind it, one would suspect a strange phenomenon occurring.</p>

<p>They both stopped eating in surprise, and then Jett exclaimed:</p>

<p>"Corbeau, it's a Pokémon!"</p>

<p>Jett reached down to the tail, and in an instant the invisible Pokémon appeared to startle and dropped the food as it came fully into view.</p>

<p>It was a small white fox. The tip of its bushy tail and ruff were adorned with scarlet fur, which glowed almost eerily with wisps of energy twirling like ethereal smoke.</p>

<p>"What an exquisite creature!" Jett exclaimed admiringly. "Why, if you wanted some meat, you didn't need to steal it; I'd share with such a charming little fox."</p>

<p>The fox Pokémon gave a happy yap.</p>

<p>"It looks like a Zorua," commented Corbeau. "But I've never seen a white Zorua...I wonder if it's a shiny one."</p>

<p>"Wait a second," said Jett, "I think I recognize this Pokémon. It came up to me a day or two ago, right in front of the Quasartico office building."</p>

<p>"Is that so?"</p>

<p>"Yes, I assumed it had wandered out of Wild Zone 7."</p>

<p>Corbeau opened a Pokédex and pointed it at the creature as it now ate. Information flashed onto the display, together with a photograph – an apparently very old photograph, in grainy black-and-white hues – showing a fox just like it in snowy woodland.</p>

<p><i>Zorua<br />
—Regional form: Hisuian—<br />
Type: Normal-Ghost<br />
Sinnohan folklore states these Pokémon sense terror and reflect it in flares of energy. Because of this, they were shunned by ancient man as an omen of death. This subspecies is thought to be extinct.</i></p>

<p>"Apparently they're not as extinct as people think," Jett commented, petting the little fox.</p>

<p>"The entry reads more like something you'd find for a Legendary," added Corbeau. "This says the last sighting for this form of Zorua was over three hundred years ago. I must say though, ghost type Pokémon always get so maligned by superstition... I have a Gastly myself, and it's one of the tamest Pokémon in my menagerie."</p>

<p>"But what's a rare Pokémon from Sinnoh doing all the way in Kalos, and in the middle of a big city like Lumiose, besides?" Jett looked down as the fox jumped onto her lap, which looked back up at her with its wide amber eyes. "You can't just be on holiday, can you?"</p>

<p>Zorua gave a yap in reply.</p>

<p>"We're going to have to find a translator," Corbeau said, "Or...we'll have to learn Pokémon language." They both laughed at this thought.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Zorua hopped onto an adjacent chair where Jett had placed her purse and began pawing at it.</p>

<p>"I wonder if it might go into a Poké Ball on its own?" Jett wondered out loud. Having said this, she took an empty Poké Ball out of her purse and placed it on the table near the Zorua.</p>

<p>Zorua yapped cheerfully and poked at the Poké Ball with its nose. There was a flash of light as the sphere opened and the little fox was swept into it.</p>

<p>"Well!" said Jett happily, "That was easy enough!"</p>

<p>"Congratulations," Corbeau smiled, "you've caught a Zorua."</p>

<p>"It caught itself," Jett replied, still perplexed at the turn of events but nonetheless happy.</p>

<p>Neither one of them noticed the small party of people on the table to the far wall across from them, who were watching Corbeau and Jett closely. They had seen everything that had transpired;  however, they were discreet enough not to be perceived themselves. They looked unexceptional – a small business gathering, perhaps, accompanied by a gong-like Pokémon called a Bronzor that floated beside them – distinguished only by the small, gold pin each one wore on their lapel.</p>

<p>Now seeing this interaction with the Zorua caused them to exchange a unanimous glance. With a few quiet murmurs and nods to each other, they paid and swiftly left the restaurant.</p>

<p>Corbeau paid the bill and as they both got up to leave, Jett said, "I think I'll let the little fox out so it can walk with us." With a swift motion, she pressed the button on the Poké Ball and out came the white Zorua.</p>

<p>"It's such a cute Pokémon," said Jett, petting Zorua. The animal seemed elated at all the attention. "I honestly don't see how anyone in the past could have ever seen it as an omen of death."</p>

<p>"Unfortunate cause and effect...the way most of these folktales got started I imagine," Corbeau told her. "They saw how Litwick shines brighter when it's near someone and concluded that it siphons away life energy. Or they had a nightmare and blamed it on a Drowzee eating their good dreams."</p>

<p>"That reminds me of last night. I had the most awful nightmare..." she said, visibly unsettled. "I dreamt that I was trapped in Lumiose City."</p>

<p>Corbeau lifted his eyebrows as if inquiring, and Jett briskly shook her head.</p>

<p>"I like Lumiose in real life, don't get me wrong," she hurried to say. "But the whole thing was horrendous. It felt as if I was in some mortal danger. I kept trying to get on the train headed out and no matter how many times I got on the damned train headed to the airport somehow it was as if I'd fallen asleep on the bench at the Pokémon Center, and then I'd wake up, and I kept trying and trying..."</p>

<p>"...Jett. Jett," Corbeau replied, holding her gently by the shoulders, "It was a nightmare."</p>

<p>She shuddered. "I think I've been overworking myself with the development project. Everything that's happened...what with those giant flowers...and the mega-evolved Pokémon..."</p>

<p>"Maybe you just need a vacation."</p>

<p>"Perhaps you're right."</p>

<p>"Jett...I want to help." He looked deep into her eyes as he said this and added, "We can take a vacation together, just the two of us...and just get away from everything for a while."</p>

<p> Jett smiled at him. "I'd like that very much," she answered.</p>

<p>They stepped outside with Zorua trotting alongside Jett, however, they discovered neither the limousine or the chauffeur anywhere to be found.</p>

<p>"Something's not right," observed Corbeau, "Remember I told the chauffeur to wait for us? He would never just leave without letting me know."</p>

<p>"Maybe it was an emergency," offered Jett.</p>

<p>"We'd better get out of here...fast," he said, taking her by the arm and leading her away from the front of the building.</p>

<p>"Corbeau, are you in a turf war with any other group?" Jett asked.</p>

<p>"No," he replied, "this is supposed to be peace time, actually... unless it's an old vendetta..." A worried look came over his face. "Let's hurry up and get to my office at the gym. I should have people on a night shift guarding the place."</p>

<p>They picked up their pace, hurrying down the sidewalk. The street was unusually abandoned.</p>

<p>It was when Corbeau saw the small group with the Bronzor, standing across the way at the mouth of an alley, that he broke out into a run, pulling Jett's hand in an attempt to keep together. Behind them they could hear Zorua growl as it and Bronzor exchanged brief flares of energy attacks, followed by a shout from one of the enemy trainers, and then the little fox raced ahead, turning the corner in front of them.</p>

<p><i>Why aren't they following...?</i> thought Corbeau, when he realized he couldn't hear anyone giving pursuit. He didn't dare stop to analyze it.</p>

<p>A chill hastened through the air, scoring icy talons down his spine. He spun around as a booming noise pounded across their ears, and in a heartbeat they were surrounded by a cloud of ink-black darkness.</p>

<p>Jett screamed. Corbeau didn't wait, throwing a Poké Ball to the floor and calling out orders to his Pokémon as it emerged. "Salazzle, Flamethrower!"</p>

<p>The black lizard opened its jaws and twirled about, letting flame assail in all directions. The fiery whirlwind lashed out at the encircling shadows. Somewhere in their depths, a sharp roar of pain bellowed from some unseen Pokémon, and in an instant the shadows receded.</p>

<p>That left Corbeau, his breath still tight from tension, Jett pressed close to him, and Salazzle, looking around for its vanished opponent. They were once again entirely alone.</p>

<p>Corbeau straightened himself, forcing himself to calm.</p>

<p>"Good work, Salazzle," he said, and recalled the lizard to its Poké Ball with the press of a button. "Come back."</p>

<p>"What was that...?" Jett whispered breathlessly, and as they now took in their hushed surroundings, they both noticed something else.</p>

<p>The fox had gone inexplicably missing.</p>

<p>"Where's Zorua?" she asked.</p>

<p>Before Corbeau could answer, the darkness suddenly exploded again, from behind them; something wrapped tightly around Jett's body and yanked her backwards. Her purse fell from her hands, falling to the sidewalk curb as she began shrieking and trying to get free.</p>

<p>Corbeau attempted to pull her back only for a huge tentacle to coil around his neck as if trying to strangle him, and Corbeau, now desperate, struggling himself as a wild man against the predatory Pokémon dragging them both into its oppressive darkness.</p>

<p>...and as fast as it had arisen the shadow had disappeared, and so had Corbeau, and Jett, teleporting them away to a place unknown; the only spectators remaining which had borne witness to this strange battle were the dreadful silence of desolation and the lonely little fox Pokémon cowering unseen behind the trash bin.</p>

<h2>✅Chapter 2 - Three Factions and a Fox</h2>

<p>Archer Verdi strolled along the Saison Canal at a brisk pace, unconcerned but nonetheless watchful. This wasn't Team Rocket territory; it was the Rust Syndicate's. It was always smart to be on guard when in a region they did not control.</p>

<p>Although the two crime families got along at best and were neutral at worst, it was a paradigm that could always change. The underworld could be as fluid as the dark waters of the Saison. The two dons had been supposed to have a meeting, which was inexplicably missed by Corbeau; while Giovanni did not suspect any nefarious reason for it, these slights were the sort of misunderstanding that could lead to bloodshed under the command of less astute men.</p>

<p><i>Giovanni is, however, the most astute leader Team Rocket could ever have,</i> Archer thought with pride. His reaction had been to send Archer to Lumiose City in the Kalos region and see what was going on with the Rust Syndicate. Unexpected disasters, of course, were not unheard of in a world where even the tamest woodland animal could fry someone with the voltage of a lightning bolt.</p>

<p>The day was sunlit yet chilly. Lumiose was as vibrant as ever, with people walking to and fro on the sidewalks and Archer with his trim vest-suit attire fitting in seamlessly as just another businessman. He was close to thirty, with neatly combed teal hair and refined manners that hid the lethal training expected of one of Team Rocket's elite admins.</p>

<p>As he was walking along, he glanced down absently and his eye was drawn to something in a side alley.</p>

<p><i>...is that a Poké Ball?</i></p>

<p>Archer stepped discreetly away from the crowded sidewalk into the shadows of the alleyway. A few foraging Trubbish and Ratatta darted off as he approached the small red sphere seemingly discarded on the asphalt.</p>

<p>A fully intact, albeit empty, Poké Ball. As he picked it up, he noticed a purse lying nearby that it had apparently rolled out from, and went to examine this as well. The contents of the purse were still within it, among which was a wallet that held the owner's identification.</p>

<p><i>Mrs. Jett Silph.</i></p>

<p>Archer hesitated. He knew Jett from a prior assignment in Lumiose, but she wasn't the sort of person to toss important items so senselessly.</p>

<p>As he pondered the discovery and debated what to do, the sound of something clattering made Archer glance up. He focused on the direction of the sound and spied two amber eyes watching from the shadows behind a trash bin.</p>

<p>The creature stepped tentatively into view. It was a rare Pokémon, a Zorua with white fur.</p>

<p>Archer stared at the fox. The fox stared back...and then leapt to snatch the empty Poké Ball from Archer's hand and ran for it.</p>

<p>"Damn it, come back here!" Archer exclaimed.</p>

<p>Down the alley ran Zorua, clutching the Poké Ball in its jaws, with Archer chasing it. They raced all the way to the opposite street, where Archer nearly skidded to avoid colliding with passers-by as the fox swerved about and darted nimbly around the corner.</p>

<p>He didn't let up, and his own attempt to round the corner faltered when he suddenly ended up face-to-face with a woman about his age. She blinked, gazing into his eyes with surprise. Trotting about her ankles was a Flareon, a fluffy fire type cat-fox with orange fur, that looked up at Archer with indifference.</p>

<p>Zorua had seemingly vanished.</p>

<p>"Have you seen a Pokémon run this way?" Archer asked urgently.</p>

<p>The lady didn't get a chance to say anything. There was a loud yap that made them both look up.</p>

<p>Archer saw the fox now, perched on the edge of the rooftop above, bouncing around as if to attract their attention. Zorua then scooped up the Poké Ball from where the capsule had been placed beside it as it took off running again across the building.</p>

<p>Archer didn't hesitate; he dashed after it. Into a side alleyway, now using all his Team Rocket training to effect, he leapt from the lid of a trash bin to a fire escape and easily reached the rooftop in time for Zorua to flash by in a streak of white fur and flickers of red energy.</p>

<p>To his surprise, the woman followed him. She easily met his limber gait, emulating his unorthodox path up to land lightly beside Archer on the rooftop.</p>

<p>The Flareon bounded up beside her as she pointed towards the runaway fox. "Flareon – corner that Zorua!"</p>

<p>Archer turned towards her sharply as Flareon flattened its ears and darted towards their quarry. "That Pokémon is mine," he said in an acerbic way.</p>

<p>"You could use a little help pinning it down," she replied without any animosity.</p>

<p>Flareon lunged at the Zorua, which went suddenly invisible and in an instant appeared from behind to tackle the fire type. Flareon barely seemed to take damage at all by the attack, but it was enough for Zorua to take off running past it.</p>

<p>Archer, not to be outdone, retrieved a Poké Ball and with the press of a button sent out his own Pokémon. The creature, a sleek black dog with horns like those of an antelope, emerged in a flare of light. "Houndoom, Bite! Get ahold of Zorua!"</p>

<p>Houndoom responded to Archer's orders immediately; it tried to seize Zorua in its jaws, but the fox spun about and counter-attacked with a burst of flaring energy. When Houndoom yelped and sprang away, Zorua leapt to the opposing building – still carrying the Poké Ball.</p>

<p>After Zorua went both Flareon and Houndoom, and after them went their trainers, each as lithe and fast as the other.</p>

<p>By now Archer was starting to wonder in earnest about the lady. <i>She's not Team Rocket,</i> Archer thought, <i>but she certainly seems comparably trained.</i></p>

<p>"Are you from the Rust Syndicate?" he asked at a moment when they landed on a platform and he caught his breath.</p>

<p>"Rust Syndicate? What's that?" she countered innocuously.</p>

<p>The Zorua bolted to the ground, zipping across the street and ending up at a large office building framed by obsidian walls. Archer recognized it immediately as the headquarters of Rust Industries, as well as the current location of the Lumiose gym.</p>

<p>Archer and the woman, along with their two Pokémon, reached the entrance to the building in time to see the fox cross the enclosed front garden and reach the doorway. Zorua turned and looked back at them expectantly.</p>

<p>The two guards at the entrance, who were undoubtedly Rust Syndicate men, did not appear to have seen the Zorua. They stepped before Archer and his tagalog companion to bar their path.</p>

<p>Archer spoke first. "You have a fox in there. It's mine."</p>

<p>"What fox?" asked the guard.</p>

<p>"That fox, right there."</p>

<p>They looked towards where he was pointing. "There's nothing there," replied the guard, turning back.</p>

<p>Zorua was squinting mischievously, still sitting in plain sight. Archer scowled, realizing that unless they were being intentionally obtuse, it was using some sort of ability to cloak itself from the guards. <i>This kind of Zorua must be a ghost type if it can pull off that kind of trick.</i></p>

<p>"The building is closed," said one of the guards, tearing Archer's attention away from the smug Zorua.</p>

<p><i>Stupid. The Rust Syndicate always keeps this place staffed. To say nothing of how the Pokémon League office has to be open during typical working hours.</i> Archer didn't bother pointing out the inconsistency, however.</p>

<p>"I'm from Rocket Enterprises," he replied tersely. "Now if you don't mind, I'll take my fox."</p>

<p>"There's no fox, and you're not getting by here."</p>

<p>Archer had finally had it.</p>

<p>With a sudden motion he'd knocked the first man against the wall, and Houndoom recognizing its cue leapt snarling to pin the other down onto the ground.</p>

<p>"Tell your boss not to employ dimwits at the gates," Archer snapped. "It makes your whole operation look slipshod."</p>

<p>He didn't pay heed to the lady, who sprinted past them and headed for Zorua. The fox Pokémon flattened its ears and scampered evasively past her, directly to Archer. It took a little jump onto his shoulders and sat there, completely nonchalant.</p>

<p>"...hey, where'd that fox come from?" the guard now uttered, still fairly stunned.</p>

<p>"I'd be grateful," a new voice broke in gruffly, "if you'd release the guards. They're new recruits still learning the ropes."</p>

<p>Archer glanced up to see the speaker approach, having come out from the building. He was heavyset and imposing, with salt-and-pepper hair bearing sideburns, and dressed in a pinstriped suit with a golden tie.</p>

<p>"Philippe," Archer acknowledged him, immediately recognizing the second-in-command of the Rust Syndicate. "You have perfect timing."</p>

<p>Philippe then turned towards the woman, and his eyebrows lifted. "Cynthia? This is a big surprise. What brings the Sinnoh region's champion to our fine city?"</p>

<p>Archer looked over with even greater surprise. She seemed vaguely familiar, yes, now that he really looked at her, but it was true that when it came to sports he didn't follow the Sinnoh League battles so much as the matches from Kanto and Johto. He <i>had</i> heard that the current head of Sinnoh's Elite Four was one of the most skilled trainers the region had seen in recent years, nothing more.</p>

<p><i>If I'd have known she looked like that, I'd have been watching the Sinnoh battles more closely,</i> he reflected.</p>

<p>Cynthia was blonde, with long hair that fell almost to her waist and a slender, supple figure. Her black, fur-trimmed attire aligned with her silhouette, and a plunging neckline revealed nothing yet insinuated much. Distinctive, teardrop-shaped hairpins caught the sunlight with an onyx shine only hinting at how much they undoubtedly glittered at night. It was her eyes, however, that were especially striking; they held a lucidity like mother-of-pearl, an almost prismatic brilliance of grey.</p>

<p>"I had some business at the Lumiose museum," Cynthia said, with a delicate smile. "Of course, when I saw this runaway Zorua, I couldn't help but take an interest. That's a Hisuian Zorua, a very special Pokémon."</p>

<p>Zorua meanwhile had started poking at Archer with a paw. Archer noticed something small and shiny it held in its mouth and took it while Philippe and Cynthia were distracted with the conversation.</p>

<p>It was a gold pin, the arcing shape of a letter G.</p>

<p>"Where'd you get this, I wonder?" Archer murmured, but he quickly tucked it away before anyone else could notice.</p>

<p>He retrieved the Poké Ball he'd found with Jett's purse and opened it. A flare of red light engulfed Zorua and the fox Pokémon vanished into the capsule.</p>

<p><i>Well then,</i> Archer thought, turning back towards Philippe and Cynthia, <i>it looks like Zorua belongs to Jett. None of which explains why it was out in an alleyway with Jett's possessions strewn about. I wonder if something happened to her?</i></p>

<p>"You must be a very special trainer in turn, if you've gained the trust of that Zorua." Cynthia's voice, bearing a certain reflective curiosity, broke into his thoughts.</p>

<p>"Who can tell what a Pokémon is thinking," said Archer modestly. He was eager to sidestep the conversation.</p>

<p>Cynthia seemed to perceive it, though perhaps she misjudged the reason. "Let me explain. Outside of my position at the League, I used to work as an archeologist, with a specialty in ancient mythology. Many of those myths, you see, turn out to be very true." She shook his hand warmly. "I'm sure we'll meet again, Mr...?"</p>

<p>"Archer Verdi. Just call me Archer."</p>

<p>"Archer, then."</p>

<p>Cynthia stepped away with the Flareon dutifully following at her heels.</p>

<p>She had a certain grace, a composed elegance, to the way she carried herself. That lithe figure and the way her clothing brought out the curves only helped. Archer was watching her leave when Philippe spoke up.</p>

<p>"I was waiting for her to leave," Philippe said, "no doubt you're here on behalf of your boss, on account of that missed video conference we of the Rust Syndicate were set to have with your organization."</p>

<p>"Team Rocket does understand it to be unusual for Mr. Corbeau," affirmed Archer. "He has always been punctual in these affairs."</p>

<p>"Truth is Mr. Corbeau is missing. He went to dinner with Mrs. Silph and hasn't come back."</p>

<p>"I see," Archer said dryly, "I will notify Giovanni immediately."</p>

<p>"We are not sure how to proceed," Philippe added, with complete honesty, "Mr. Corbeau makes all decisions at the Rust Syndicate. We went by both his residence and hers, incidentally. They are nowhere to be found."</p>

<p>There was unmistakable worry there. Archer noticed how they must be truly at a loss if they were willing to throw themselves at the aid of another syndicate this way. It left them possibly open to attack, though he knew as well as they that Giovanni would likely not do so. Team Rocket had no animosity towards the Rust Syndicate.</p>

<p>"We might contact you again regarding this," Archer told him, "after I speak to Giovanni; he will say the next step."</p>

<p>He turned and left the building. Archer glanced at his wristwatch. <i>I can have lunch and contact the boss at the same time,</i> he thought.</p><hr>

<p>Café Gallant was on the corner street across from the Rust Syndicate's headquarters.</p>

<p>It was already nearing noontime so Archer figured the best course of action would be to have lunch and then catch the next flight heading back out to the Kanto region. His meal order was swiftly filled, whereupon he quietly selected a corner table with an unobstructed view of the entrance and the street outside. Small cautions were always advisable in his line of work, and were never omitted by Archer; certainly he had his reasons, and bad experiences, to be so guarded.</p>

<p>He informed Giovanni about the strange disappearance of Corbeau and Jett via video-phone.</p>

<p>Giovanni Delavita – the businessman who owned the now-wealthiest conglomerate in the world, Rocket Enterprises, and the gym leader of Viridian City in the Kanto region. All of it was secondary, of course, to his more illicit occupation as the don of Team Rocket. Persian, the large, cream-furred cat Pokémon that almost perpetually lounged on his lap, acted as <i>consigliere</i>.</p>

<p>"An enigma," said Giovanni, after Archer had finished recounting what had transpired. "It could be a sign of a power struggle within the Rust Syndicate. Either that or one of the other organizations have decided to make a move into Rust's territory."</p>

<p>"My money's on the latter." Archer showed Giovanni the gold letter G pin that Zorua had given him. It glistened in the overhead lights of the café. "The fox had this. Isn't this the insignia of Team Galactic?"</p>

<p>"Yes, it is." Giovanni looked grim, as if the item corroborated an unstated suspicion of his. "This all points to the start of another mafia feud, though the fact Team Galactic has the resources for this kind of power grab opens a great many questions..."</p>

<p>Persian gave a long meow. Giovanni nodded in comprehension of whatever the cat Pokémon had said and turned his attention back towards Archer.</p>

<p>"Return to headquarters. We'll discuss this more in depth." Without another word, the don of Team Rocket hung up the call.</p>

<p>Putting the gold pin back in his pocket, Archer returned to his meal. <i>Hopefully whatever is happening isn't poised to cause problems for our own business operations,</i> he thought, with a glance outside at the street.</p>

<p>Archer noticed Zorua's Poké Ball was shaking. He held the sphere in the palm of his hand as he addressed the Pokémon within.</p>

<p>"All right Zorua, you won't try to make another break for it if I let you out of here, will you?"</p>

<p>The Poké Ball continued to shake.</p>

<p>Archer pressed the button on the Poké Ball, unlocking the sphere into its larger size, and pressed it again to send out the Pokémon. Zorua emerged to sit before him on the table.</p>

<p>He offered the fox a piece of meat from his sandwich, which it readily took.</p>

<p>"I wonder if you know what happened to your trainer Jett," Archer speculated aloud.</p>

<p>Zorua angled an ear as it ate.</p>

<p>"When we get back to Team Rocket headquarters," Archer went on talking, "you can tell us. We have translators there..." It occurred to him briefly the pointlessness of his present monologue; all Pokémon could understand human speech perfectly, but humans for the most part had to rely either on nonverbal cues or the aid of that rare individual who could speak both languages. He would not have access to the latter till he returned to Kanto.</p>

<p>Nevertheless, Zorua showed signs of wishing to establish a conversation. Or perhaps something had caught its eye, because all of a sudden it became alert and then nudged at Archer's pocket.</p>

<p>He reached in and held out the mysterious gold pin. Zorua pawed at it and turned about, barking.</p>

<p>Archer tried to figure out what Zorua wanted to say. "Is there something you want to show me?"</p>

<p>The Zorua began yapping incessantly and pointing with a paw as if trying to attract his attention to something. Glancing up, Archer noticed a man at the counter buying a sandwich and a cup of coffee.</p>

<p>"Was that who you got this pin from?" Archer deduced. "That man over there?"</p>

<p>The fox was bouncing around. Something told Archer that he had guessed correctly.</p>

<p>Finishing the last bite of his lunch, Archer observed the man as he got his own order and walked briskly off.</p>

<p>"Let's follow him," Archer told Zorua.</p>

<h2>✅Chapter 3 - Hegemony</h2>

<p>From a distance, Archer – and the Hisuian Zorua – watched the man glance over his shoulder twice and, evidently satisfied that he had not been followed, disappear into an old warehouse.</p>

<p>They had followed him a few blocks from the café, away from the pretty business district to this dilapidated area on the city edge; an atmosphere where Archer, through his work for Team Rocket, was as comfortable in as the sparkling offices and high-rises. He crossed the street silently, Zorua scampering ahead at his heels.</p>

<p>The moment Archer stepped into the building, he felt an unnatural chill like an errant draft. Shadows extended past his steps as he followed the fox Pokémon down the corridor.</p>

<p>They emerged at a windowless room stacked with crates on hardwood flooring, with an oaken desk and an oil painting on one side. Here, he finally confronted the presumed Team Galactic agent.</p>

<p>He had cornered him at the far wall. The man turned to face Archer, Poké Ball in hand.</p>

<p>"You're bold," he remarked, "...and stupid."</p>

<p>From the corner of his eye Archer caught a glimpse of his shadow, stretched on the wall beside him as if there would have been a light source across the way.</p>

<p>The shadow was moving.</p>

<p>Archer whipped aside as the Gengar lunged at him, materializing out of the darkness. Zorua leapt to tackle it back with a vicious growl.</p>

<p>The shadowy creature immediately turned its attention towards the other Pokémon, unleashing scythes of energy attacks at Zorua. They rushed like wind at the fox, and – like regular gusts of wind – did little more than ruffle its fur. Zorua flattened its ears.</p>

<p><i>Looks like it doesn't affect it,</i> thought Archer, impressed.</p>

<p>With another well-timed leap, Zorua sprang at the Gengar. While the two Pokémon struggled, Archer didn't wait; he sprang at its trainer.</p>

<p>The man tried to swing at Archer, but the blow was deflected expertly and he stumbled back when Archer hit him across the jaw. The battle between Zorua and Gengar had ended just as quickly, the enemy Pokémon now sprawled, knocked out, on the floor.</p>

<p>"What's Galactic doing moving into Rust territory?" Archer demanded.</p>

<p>"Wouldn't you like to know," sneered the man.</p>

<p>"I <i>intend</i> to know," Archer replied dryly. "Let's start with where Corbeau and the CEO of Quasartico are."</p>

<p>"What's it to you?"</p>

<p>"Nothing much. Except where it affects Team Rocket interests," said Archer.</p>

<p>A sudden realization and subsequent fear went through the Team Galactic agent. Archer was watching him like a wolf.</p>

<p>He jumped for the desk but Archer slammed him against the wall, and then punched him in the stomach so that he crumpled to the floor with an outcry. Archer smirked at this; fiendish delight had kindled in his eyes.</p>

<p>Zorua had padded over to stand next to Archer. A scarlet glow began to ripple strangely across Zorua's fur, like some otherworldly fire; its yellow eyes had locked on the Team Galactic operative.</p>

<p>The man had almost frantically begun pleading. "Please...don't kill me!"</p>

<p>All at once, Archer's memory flashed back to the region of Orre. He remembered restraints and cruel laughter and pain, and instantly that tremulous tinge of a thrill lost its lustre and was forcibly reined in.</p>

<p>That hesitation was more than enough for his cornered enemy to make a move; he swiftly ran past him for the door. Archer, reorienting himself too late to this, shouted at Zorua. "Get him back here!"</p>

<p>Zorua let out a flare of energy. It struck the door instead of its target, the man recalling his fainted Gengar and bolting outside in the same motion.</p>

<p>Archer scowled to himself. Zorua's odd glow had receded and the fox Pokémon was looking at him quizzically.</p>

<p>Shaking his head, Archer turned towards the desk, figuring that perhaps he might find some clue there. The Team Galactic minion <i>had</i> been trying to reach for it. He rummaged through the drawers and found them all bare except for one item – a Master Ball.</p>

<p>Archer examined the capsule closely. The violet-lidded Poké Balls were extraordinarily expensive, in the range of millions, for having an infallible capture rate. This one felt like there might be a Pokémon in it already.</p>

<p><i>Any Pokémon caught in one must be exceptionally rare and valuable,</i> reasoned Archer, <i>unless they were so stupid they hurled it at a Pidgey or something.</i></p>

<p>Curiosity compelled, he stepped aside and pressed the center button on the Master Ball; the Pokémon within emerged in glistening light.</p>

<p>It was a llama.</p>

<p>That said, it was an exceptionally beautiful llama. It had wavy, close-lying white wool that shone with moonlit iridescence, as well as an impressively hooded mane that framed both the shorter grey of its face and the creature's large green eyes. Around the midriff was a horned arc that could have easily been pure gold etched by peridots.</p>

<p>Archer studied the creature briefly and then declared, "I give up. I don't know what kind of Pokémon this is."</p>

<p>The Pokémon gave a nondescript hum as Archer flipped out a Pokédex and pointed the device at it. Within moments the corresponding entry was displayed on the screen, together with a photograph taken on some rugged mountainside:</p>

<p><i>Arceus<br />
Type: Normal<br />
Arceus are classified as a highly endangered species. According to Sinnohan mythology, this Pokémon shaped the universe.</i></p>

<p>Archer considered the Pokédex entry for a moment, then looked up dubiously at the animal chewing its cud and standing before him. The Arceus watched him back, bored.</p>

<p>"It's a <i>llama</i>," emphasized Archer.</p>

<p>The Arceus stuck out its tongue.</p>

<p>"Well...rare means valuable, in any case," decided Archer with a shrug, and he tapped at the Pokédex again. "What attacks does it have?"</p>

<p><i>Hyper Beam<br />
<small>Normal - Special - Power 150</small><br />
Judgment<br />
<small>Normal - Special - Power 100</small><br />
Recover<br />
<small>Normal - Status - N/A</small><br />
Protect<br />
<small>Normal - Status - N/A</small></i></p>

<p>"It knows Hyper Beam?" Archer remarked with surprise. "Okay...a kick-ass llama. You go straight to the boss."</p>

<p>The Arceus responded with a throaty, honking noise somewhere between a Golduck and a Gogoat as he recalled it to the Master Ball.</p><hr>

<p>Archer was back in the Kanto region by evening, with two Pokémon – the fox and the llama – as unexpected souvenirs for Giovanni.</p>

<p>Rocket Enterprises had a myriad of lucrative investments, to say nothing of a monopoly on the Poké Ball market, but the gambling business had always been first and foremost among them. Their flagship casino was the glamorous Rocket Game Corner in Celadon City, a jewel of vice and luxury.</p>

<p>Team Rocket's headquarters occupied the expansive basement level of the casino. In all of the regions they dominated, in fact, they made it a point to set up a hideout below whatever gambling den they invariably built or bought in that area. It was an easy point of reference for Team Rocket agents and since casinos were naturally heavily guarded anyway there were few questions – if anyone would be so suicidal <i>to</i> ask questions.</p>

<p>He soon found himself in Giovanni's office discussing the acquisition of the Arceus. Seizing a Legendary Pokémon was always a great prize for Team Rocket.</p>

<p>"You've done well Archer," Giovanni said, examining the Master Ball that contained Arceus, "The most interesting part of all this is that its trainer is registered as Mr. Cyrus Akari."</p>

<p>"Cyrus, the leader of Team Galactic?" remarked Archer.</p>

<p>"Yes. Of course with a conflict between Team Galactic and the Rust Syndicate ongoing, they will expect that it was one of Rust's people who took the Arceus," said Giovanni with approval. "The only person able to tell them otherwise would have been the Galactic agent you fought with. I presume you dealt with that problem when you acquired the Pokémon."</p>

<p>"Well..." stammered Archer, "I beat him up, as per usual."</p>

<p>Giovanni stared at him for about a minute and finally spoke up. "You let him go didn't you?"</p>

<p>"I didn't see the need to pursue him," Archer admitted.</p>

<p>"You're a Team Rocket agent, remember?"</p>

<p>"I know," said Archer.</p>

<p>Giovanni shook his head. "Archer, maybe you should take some time off."</p>

<p>"No sir, please!" said Archer, "I'm okay. Really I am."</p>

<p>The don of Team Rocket looked unconvinced and Archer could only cringe at his own failure. There had been days in the past when he would have been first to act and last to repent, and while he would still act in the thick of combat, it was no longer without uncertainty. He was <i>not</i> okay, not the way he saw it.</p>

<p>He glanced away sheepishly to the Hisuian Zorua, which was sitting next to Persian. The two Pokémon were having an animated conversation. Archer wondered what they were discussing.</p>

<p>Giovanni sighed. "Alright Archer," he said. "It's best to jump right back into work anyway. I've got a new assignment for you."</p>

<p>Archer straightened his posture expectantly as Giovanni placed the Master Ball aside atop his desk.</p>

<p>"Galactic evidently has gotten the finances necessary to expand their operations and try to move into the other organizations' territories. This is, of course, concerning to our own business affairs."</p>

<p>"Then we ought to pinpoint their newfound source of revenue," Archer considered.</p>

<p>"Early intelligence points to a secret investor," affirmed Giovanni, "Someone within the Sinnohan Pokémon League."</p>

<p>"One of the clerks perhaps?"</p>

<p>Giovanni shook his head. "It's a high-profile official. One of the gym leaders, most likely."</p>

<p>"What would they get out of that sort of deal?" wondered Archer.</p>

<p>"It could be anything," Giovanni replied. "We're not even sure how they're getting the funds to Team Galactic's coffers. It could be anything from a bank transfer to a Pokémon courier. Which brings me to your mission."</p>

<p>Persian padded over, entwining itself around its trainer's legs as Giovanni went on:</p>

<p>"You'll be going to the Sinnoh region tomorrow, incognito, as a journeying trainer challenging the League. This will allow you to visit each of the region's major cities and scope out Team Galactic's activities as well as learn the identity of their financier."</p>

<p>"To go to Sinnoh as a <i>gym challenger</i>?" Archer echoed.</p>

<p>The surprised note in his voice went either totally disregarded or undetected by the boss of Team Rocket. "You'll take the fox with you," added Giovanni.</p>

<p>Archer gave an askance look towards Zorua. The ghost type Pokémon was watching him intently.</p>

<p>"Are we changing the registration?" Archer asked.</p>

<p>Giovanni gave a mild gesture. "Yes – and no. If you find Jett alive, we'll return Zorua to her. But for now, Zorua is yours, and the registration will appear as if it had been traded to you. In return for us trying to reunite it with Jett, Zorua will help us look into this Team Galactic business."</p>

<p>"...how?" inquired Archer. There was no masking how dubious he was about Zorua's usefulness in this affair.</p>

<p>Zorua seemed to fade before his eyes. He glanced over at where the fox had been; mere seconds later the fox reappeared there holding something in its mouth.</p>

<p>Archer recognized his identification card. "Hey, give that back. How the hell did you get that out of my wallet?" he demanded, taking the card away from a now-laughing fox.</p>

<p>Giovanni looked content. "One of Zorua's attacks is Thief. As a ghost type, it's also able to move unseen, which makes it perfect to get into private offices and steal items without having to break in the usual ways."</p>

<p>"So I see," said Archer, now impressed. "All right Zorua, welcome to the team."</p>

<p>Zorua replied with a happy yap.</p>

<p>"You'll need to take at least two Pokémon anyway, so Zorua will be the counterpart to your Houndoom," Giovanni added. "The official matches are double battles."</p>

<p>"I thought that was only in Orre."</p>

<p>"The Sinnoh League adopted the format last year."</p>

<p>"What about Corbeau?" Archer asked, "Are we going to get involved in that?"</p>

<p>"We know Team Galactic is responsible for his disappearance," Giovanni responded noncommittally. "If you find Jett, you'll likely find Corbeau. No one has contacted the Rust Syndicate for a ransom."</p>

<p>"I'm sure Philippe is making his own inquiries," said Archer.</p>

<p>"Your job is threefold," Giovanni declared. "Figure out what Team Galactic is planning, learn who their financier is, and try to find out what became of Corbeau and Jett."</p>

<p>The Team Rocket don hesitated.</p>

<p>"And needless to say, so no one questions your moving around the region...make sure you win at the Sinnoh League."</p><hr>

<p>To make the ruse look good, they forged badges from the Kanto League circuit. The cover was thus; Archer had journeyed through Kanto, winning at four of the gyms before having to drop his challenge on account of personal affairs. Now that he was older, he decided to try again, attempting a fresh run in Sinnoh instead of picking up where he'd left off.</p>

<p>There was a certain inherent novelty in this, being that he was a Team Rocket admin. His father had been the <i>consigliere</i> for a then-nascent Team Rocket, killed in the crossfire of one of their early mob wars; his mother had followed shortly thereafter, leaving him orphaned at a young age to be raised by his godfather Giovanni – for all intents and purposes, under the shadow of their organization.</p>

<p>Upon coming of age most people received their starter Pokémon. This had been so with Archer, who had been gifted Houndoom in the form of a Houndour puppy from Giovanni, but this was where the similarity ended. Whereas most people, as a sort of rite of passage, then went on a journey with their Pokémon to collect gym badges and see the region, Archer had instead begun his cadet training in earnest.</p>

<p>While the average trainer became proficient in sport battling, a Team Rocket agent became an expert in the fine art of military combat. There was no urgency in civilian training – gym badges did not expire, so the only penalty for failure was being unable to take part in that season's League tournament. For a Team Rocket agent, there was always urgency – at its best, the urgency of skirting the law and outwitting it; at its worst, an urgency somewhere in the realm of life and death. The pressure mounted when Archer had been promoted to the rank of admin, an elite position granted to only the most trusted of the crime syndicate's members.</p>

<p>So Archer had not expected to ever go on a traditional trainer's journey and looked on the practice like a curious outsider, as a facet of a lifestyle he would never personally experience yet did not feel any sort of longing for.</p>

<p>That sentiment of indifference, however, was not shared by every admin.</p>

<p>"Damn, you're going on a real Pokémon trainer's journey," said Jessie, while Archer was packing for the trip.</p>

<p>"You're lucky!" added James.</p>

<p>Archer looked up from the luggage, mystified. "What?"</p>

<p>"We'd kill for the chance to just travel around with our Pokémon and live like normal civilians for a change," Jessie admitted.</p>

<p>"Don't know who, though," James conceded.</p>

<p>"Maybe I'm missing something. You're Giovanni's right-hand guards and get all the best assignments and meanwhile I'm here playing sports as an excuse to rifle through bank paperwork. I think you've got the allocation of luck mixed up," Archer said genially.</p>

<p>The third member of their trio, the cat Meowth, hopped up on James' shoulder and yowled, "That depends on your idea of good luck!"</p>

<p>"I guess." Archer turned his attention back to the luggage as Meowth jumped down and stood up on his hind paws to peer into the bag. The golden horn on the Pokémon's head shone like a coin.</p>

<p>"You need to pack some fish. You should never go on a trip without cans of seafood," the cat meowed.</p>

<p>"What's on your mind, Archer?" asked James, when Archer merely dismissed Meowth's comment with a little wave. "<i>Something</i> has been on your mind lately."</p>

<p>Jessie spoke up. "Can I speak frankly?"</p>

<p>Archer returned a sideways look. "Will anything I say stop you?"</p>

<p>"You haven't been the same since what happened to you in Orre."</p>

<p>He hesitated.</p>

<p>"We get it," Jessie went on, "I don't think anyone could be the same after being held prisoner in those conditions...but what I'm talking about is the way you approach your work. You hold some part of yourself back now. It's like you're trying to be something you're not, and it's starting to weigh on you."</p>
